THE IDEA

SILENT POWER

Spectre was conceived as an electric Rolls-Royce without compromising the marque's fundamental character: serenity, effortless performance, comfort and exceptional craftsmanship. Its electric architecture places the battery beneath the floor, contributing to an extremely smooth underfloor profile and helping create a quiet, cocoon-like passenger environment.

The long, dramatic proportions of the coupé, the fastback silhouette and the carefully controlled surfaces give Spectre a distinctive presence while remaining unmistakably Rolls-Royce.

TECHNOLOGY

ELECTRIC DRIVETRAIN

Spectre uses a dual-motor electric drivetrain designed to combine immediate response with the effortless character expected from Rolls-Royce.

MAGIC CARPET RIDE

The Architecture of Luxury platform is engineered around comfort and composure, creating the famous smooth and isolated Rolls-Royce driving experience.

BATTERY

The battery has a net capacity of approximately 102 kWh. Its position beneath the vehicle also contributes significant acoustic insulation.

CHARGING

Series II introduces improved battery technology and reduced charging times compared with the earlier Spectre specification.

AERODYNAMICS

The dramatically raked windscreen and smooth fastback surfaces contribute to Spectre's refined aerodynamic character.

BESPOKE

Rolls-Royce offers extensive personalisation, allowing clients to specify unique colours, materials, finishes and individual details.
